Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Olympia

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
