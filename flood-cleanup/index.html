Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Olympia Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Olympia and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Olympia

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
