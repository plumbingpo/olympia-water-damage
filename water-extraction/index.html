Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Olympia Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Olympia using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Why Professional Water Extraction Matters

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
